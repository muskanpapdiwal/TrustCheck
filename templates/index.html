Forensic Indicators

What We Look For

Our detection engine disaggregates risk into clear linguistic and behavioral patterns.

Language Patterns

Detects repetitive superlatives ("best ever", "life changing") and excessive exclamation marks typical of automated bot generation.

Repeated Text

Catches syndicated review templates and copy-paste astroturfing campaigns reused across multiple buyer accounts.

Rating Spikes

Identifies artificial rating inflation where rapid bursts of 5-star ratings mask genuine negative customer feedback.

Reviewer Behavior

Flags reviews lacking realistic product specifics or situational context that verified human purchasers naturally include.

Timing Anomalies

Flags suspicious clusters of reviews submitted in narrow temporal windows designed to manipulate storefront algorithms.

Incentivized Wording

Uncovers subtle commercial disclosures, free product exchanges, and paid promotional praise disguised as impartial feedback.

Transparency Note: TrustCheck scores are probabilistic analytical indicators based on linguistic and statistical patterns, not legal proof of fraud. Use them as an informed decision aid.
