The 4 Explainable Risk Spectrums

Unlike black-box models, TrustCheck provides full transparency into why a review was flagged.

1. Language Pattern Risk
Linguistic Vector

Analyzes syntactic repetition, excessive capitalization, exclamation mark bursts, and the density of promotional superlatives ("AMAZING", "MUST BUY", "10/10"). Automated generators lack natural narrative cadence.

2. Behavioral Anomaly Risk
Temporal Vector

Monitors posting velocity and sudden temporal clusters. Coordinated manipulation campaigns typically submit tens of glowing 5-star reviews within a 30–60 minute window to temporarily game storefront ranking algorithms.

3. Semantic Similarity Risk
Cosine Overlap

Identifies syndicated review templates copied across different user accounts. Reviews displaying greater than 85% cosine similarity to existing cluster centroids are immediately flagged as astroturfed content.

4. Sentiment Deviation Risk
Polarity Mismatch

Contrasts numerical star ratings against the emotional polarity of the written review. Detects artificial 5-star padding where brief generic praise contradicts genuine product feature feedback.
