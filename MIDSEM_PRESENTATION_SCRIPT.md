## Slide 3: Introduction

### What is on the slide:
- **Why Reviews Matter**: 93% of online buyers check star ratings before buying.
- **The Fake Review Problem**: Sellers buy fake 5-star reviews from bots and paid groups to boost rankings.
- **The Harm**: Buyers waste money on poor-quality or damaged products.
- **Our Solution (TrustCheck)**: An AI web tool that scans reviews, labels them as Genuine or Fake, and computes a 0–100 Trust Score.

### What to say to the teacher:
> *"Online shopping has grown exponentially, and today, more than 90% of buyers look at star ratings before making a purchase. However, many sellers flood their products with fake 5-star reviews generated by computer bots or paid syndicates.*
>
> *Because these fake reviews sound convincing, normal customers cannot spot them and end up buying substandard items. Our project, **TrustCheck**, solves this by using Machine Learning to analyze customer reviews, detect fake praise, and give the customer an easy-to-understand Trust Score."*

---

## Slide 4: Problem Statement / List of Objectives

### What is on the slide:
- **Problem Statement**: E-commerce sites just calculate a simple average of all reviews. Fake 5-star reviews easily trick the system, and normal buyers have no way of knowing the true rating.
- **Objectives**:
  1. Train an accurate Machine Learning model to classify reviews as Genuine or Fake.
  2. Create a mathematical Trust Score (0 to 100).
  3. Reveal the adjusted, real product rating after removing fake reviews.
  4. Build an easy-to-use web dashboard with direct product link scraping.

### What to say to the teacher:
> *"The core problem is that websites like Amazon calculate a simple unweighted average of all posted reviews. If a seller adds 100 fake 5-star reviews, the rating jumps to 4.8 stars even if the product is poor.*
>
> *Our objectives are: First, train an AI model to detect fake reviews. Second, calculate a Trust Score from 0 to 100 showing how reliable the product is. Third, reveal the adjusted real rating by taking the average of only genuine reviews. And fourth, deploy an easy web dashboard for everyday shoppers."*

---

## Slide 5: Dataset Description

### What is on the slide:
- **Dataset**: Kaggle Fake Reviews Dataset (40,432 customer reviews).
- **Two Balanced Classes**:
  - 50% Genuine Reviews (`OR` - Original Reviews by real buyers)
  - 50% Fake Reviews (`CG` - Computer-Generated fake reviews)
- **Categories**: Electronics, Home & Kitchen, Sports, Books, Clothing.
- **Text Preprocessing**: Lowercasing, removing punctuation, stop-word filtering, and TF-IDF feature extraction (5,000 words).
- **Data Split**: 80% for Training (32,345 reviews) and 20% for Testing (8,087 reviews).

### What to say to the teacher:
> *"For training our model, we used the Kaggle Fake Reviews Dataset containing over 40,000 labeled reviews across categories like Electronics, Home appliances, and Books.*
>
> *The dataset is perfectly balanced with 50% genuine reviews written by real buyers and 50% fake reviews generated by computer algorithms.*
>
> *We cleaned the text by removing punctuation, converted it to lowercase, removed common stop words like 'the' and 'is', and converted words into numbers using TF-IDF with unigrams and bigrams. We trained our model on 80% of the data and tested it on the remaining 20%."*

## Slide 6: Design and Methodology

### What is on the slide:
- **Algorithms Compared**:
  - Multinomial Naive Bayes: 85.2% Accuracy
  - **Logistic Regression: 89.4% Accuracy (Selected Champion)**
- **Why Logistic Regression?**: Higher accuracy and fewer false alarms on honest reviews.
- **Trust Score Formula**:
  $$\text{Trust Score} = 100 - \left(\frac{\% \text{ Fake} \times \text{Confidence}}{100}\right)$$
- **4-Step Architecture**:
  1. Input (URL, CSV, or pasted text)
  2. Text Preprocessing (TF-IDF vectorizer)
  3. ML Prediction (Genuine or Fake with confidence %)
  4. Interactive Dashboard (Trust gauge & adjusted rating)

### What to say to the teacher:
> *"We compared two popular algorithms on the exact same dataset: Naive Bayes gave 85% accuracy, but **Logistic Regression achieved 89.4% accuracy** with fewer false alarms, so we selected it for production.*
>
> *We also designed a dynamic Trust Score formula: it starts at 100, and subtracts points based on both how many fake reviews exist AND how confident the model is.*
>
> *The system flow has 4 simple steps: The user inputs reviews or an Amazon link; the text is vectorized; the ML model predicts each review; and the dashboard displays the results in under one second."*

## Slide 7: Research Contributions

### What is on the slide:
- **1. Dual-Rating Comparison**: Shows the advertised rating side-by-side with the Genuine-Only rating to expose inflation.
- **2. 4-Vector Review Forensics**: Explains *why* a review is fake (Spam words, fake sentiment, repeated template, missing details).
- **3. Automatic Web Scraper**: Directly fetches customer reviews from Amazon and Flipkart product URLs.
- **4. Deployed Live Website**: Completely working web application running live on Vercel (`trust-check-rouge.vercel.app`).

### What to say to the teacher:
> *"Here is what makes TrustCheck unique: First, we show the advertised rating right next to the Genuine-Only rating. For example, a product may advertise 4.6 stars, but after removing fake reviews, its real rating is only 3.8 stars.*
>
> *Second, we don't just say 'Fake' — we explain why through 4 risk factors: spam words, template repetition, fake sentiment, and lack of product details.*
>
> *Third, we have built a live web scraper for Amazon and Flipkart links. And fourth, our project is fully deployed and accessible live online for anyone to test."*
